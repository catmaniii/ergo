# 行为准则

本准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。

## 我们的承诺

作为成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺：不论年龄、体型、是否残障、族裔、性征、性别认同和表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向，参与本项目的每个人都不会受到骚扰。我们会以开放、友好、包容、健康的方式交流。

## 我们的标准

有助于营造良好环境的做法：

- 对别人体贴、友善；
- 尊重不同的观点和经验；
- 给出建设性的意见，也坦然接受；
- 为自己的错误负责、向受影响的人道歉，并从中学习；
- 关注对整个社区最好的结果，而不只是对自己最好的。

不能接受的行为：

- 带有性意味的言语或图像，任何形式的性关注或性骚扰；
- 恶意挑衅、侮辱或贬损的评论，人身攻击或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下的骚扰；
- 未经明确许可公开他人的私人信息（比如住址、邮箱）；
- 其他在专业场合下会被合理认为不恰当的行为。

## 维护者的责任

维护者负责解释和执行这份准则，可以删除、修改或拒绝不符合准则的评论、提交、代码、wiki 编辑、issue 和其他贡献，并会说明理由。

## 适用范围

适用于本项目的所有场合（仓库、issue、PR、讨论区），也适用于代表本项目在公共场合的言行。

## 报告

遇到违反准则的情况，请在本仓库的 **Security** 页面点 **Report a vulnerability** 私下报告，标题写明"行为准则"。维护者会及时、公正地处理，并对报告人的隐私和安全保密。

## 处理方式

维护者按情节轻重采取下列措施：

1. **提醒**：私下书面提醒，说明哪里不合适；可能要求公开道歉。
2. **警告**：一段时间内不得与相关的人互动；再犯会受更重的处理。
3. **暂时封禁**：一段时间内不得以任何方式参与社区。
4. **永久封禁**：持续违反准则、骚扰他人、攻击或贬低某一群体的，永久不得参与社区。

# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the overall community, not just for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions), and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

To report a violation, use **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's **Security** page and put "Code of Conduct" in the title, so the report stays private. Maintainers will review and investigate all complaints promptly and fairly, and will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate; a public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: no interaction with the people involved for a specified period; further violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban**: no interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban**: for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.
